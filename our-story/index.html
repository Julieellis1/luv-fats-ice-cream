Our story

It started with a birthday.

Chi Ndika grew up in Austin with a nutritionist mom and no ice cream in the freezer. Years later, when her mom couldn’t have dairy, Chi set out to make a scoop worth celebrating with.

The texture she was chasing

Chi calls herself a big textural eater. Most vegan ice cream she tried was too icy, or tasted only of the milk it was made from. So she went looking for other fats, starting with her mom’s favorite fruit: the avocado.

“I was looking for other fats to incorporate into the ice cream.”

Chi Ndika, to KUT’s Black Austin Matters, 2025

The result is a base of coconut milk, avocado, a little cocoa butter and avocado oil, churned in small batches on a machine named Emery. Her flavors are inspired by her Kenyan and Nigerian roots, and by fresh produce, herbs and nuts from Austin’s farmers markets.

Luv Fats is Black family-run and woman-owned. It started as a gift, and it’s still made like one.

The timeline

  1. 2016

    A birthday problem

    Chi’s mom had developed a dairy sensitivity and still deserved a birthday dessert. Chi started experimenting.

  2. 2018

    Farmers markets

    Luv Fats pints show up at Austin farmers markets, including Sundays at Mueller.

  3. 2022

    The pink window

    On May 27, a walk-up window opens at 7135 N Lamar, and the hot-pink shop becomes a neighborhood landmark.

  4. 2025

    Between homes

    The Lamar shop closes. Chi raises funds for a new space and keeps going with pop-ups and pint orders.

  5. 2026

    Loyola Lane

    On July 29, Luv Fats opens in its new pink-painted home at 4700 Loyola Ln.
